The Kurukshetra War: A Data-Driven Introduction

📜

The Kurukshetra War, fought around 3139 BCE, was one of the largest and deadliest battles in history, recorded in the Mahabharata. It was a war of dharma (righteousness) vs. adharma (unrighteousness), where the Pandavas (5 brothers), led by Yudhishthira, fought against their 100 Kaurava cousins, led by Duryodhana, for the throne of Hastinapura.

The Cause of the War

📜
  • King Dhritarashtra's favoritism led Duryodhana to deny the Pandavas their rightful kingdom.
  • The infamous dice game resulted in the Pandavas' 13-year exile after being deceived.
  • Duryodhana refused peace proposals, choosing war despite Krishna's final attempts for a settlement.

The War's Legacy

📕
Bhagavad Gita was delivered by Krishna to Arjuna before the war
🏛️
Mass destruction led to the fall of the Kauravas, ending their dynasty
👑
Pandavas ruled for 36 years, before renouncing their kingdom and journeying toward salvation
💭

This war wasn't just a clash of armies but a battle of ideologies, shaping the future of Bharata (India).
