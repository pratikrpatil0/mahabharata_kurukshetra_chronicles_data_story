Ancient Data, Modern Storytelling

"Transforming the epic Kurukshetra War from legend to data visualization - A journey through information design"

Project Overview

📊

This data story project transforms the ancient 18-day Kurukshetra War from the Mahabharata into a comprehensive data visualization experience. By converting narrative elements into measurable data points, I've created a thematic presentation that helps modern audiences understand the scale, impact, and significance of one of history's most legendary conflicts.
