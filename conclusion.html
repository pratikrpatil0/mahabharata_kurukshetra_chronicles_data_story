War Casualties and Survival Rate

📉

The Kurukshetra War wiped out 99% of all combatants, making it one of the most devastating battles in history. From the initial 4 million warriors, only 15 survived to see the war's end.

Political and Social Impact

⚔️
  • End of the Kuru Dynasty: Nearly all Kaurava and Pandava heirs perished, leading to a political vacuum in Hastinapura.
  • Mass Depopulation: The war resulted in a dramatic decline in military-aged men across Bharata (India).
  • Economic Collapse: The agricultural and trade systems suffered as labor forces were lost.

Final Thoughts: The War's Legacy

🕉️
  • 99.5% warrior fatality rate made it the deadliest war of ancient India.
  • Hastinapura was left without a strong ruler, leading to a shift in power dynamics.
  • Mahabharata's war remains a cautionary tale on the costs of greed, power, and revenge.
💭

The Kurukshetra War was not just a battle of arrows and swords but of ideologies and destiny, shaping the future of Bharata for centuries.
