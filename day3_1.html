Key Battle Insight
Despite Bhishma's devastating performance slaying 50,000 soldiers alone, this day marked the strategic emergence of Shikhandi—positioned to counter the invincible Bhishma. This development, orchestrated by Krishna, would ultimately determine the course of the entire war.
