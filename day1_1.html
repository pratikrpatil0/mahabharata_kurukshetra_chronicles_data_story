Strategic Analysis
On Day 1, the Pandavas deployed the Krauncha (Heron) formation for offensive capability, while the Kauravas countered with the Vajra (Diamond) formation for defense. Bhishma Pitamaha, despite being over 120 years old, led the Kaurava forces with unmatched skill, while the younger Dhrishtadyumna commanded the Pandava army with caution in his first day of engagement.
