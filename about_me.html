Meet the Developer

👋

Hello! I'm Pratik Patil, a third-year student at FLAME University with a passion for creative data visualization and digital storytelling. I developed "The Kurukshetra Chronicles" as part of my Information and Data Visualization course project, transforming the ancient epic into an interactive visual experience.

🎓
Currently pursuing my undergraduate degree at FLAME University, where I'm developing skills in design thinking, data science, and interactive media.
💻
Passionate about combining technology with storytelling to create immersive data-driven experiences that educate and engage users.
🔍
Fascinated by how ancient narratives can be reimagined through modern visualization techniques, creating new ways to understand historical and cultural stories.
